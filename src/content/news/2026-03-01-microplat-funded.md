title: MICROPLAT funded by FAPESP
date: 2026-03-01
tags: [grant, project]
---

The JUST Lab's project **MICROPLAT — Plataforma de Desenvolvimento de Biofármaco
em Microalgas: Creglicerase** has been funded by FAPESP (process 2025/09133-0),
running from March 2026 to February 2031. The project develops a microalgal
platform for biopharmaceutical production, with associated researchers Adalberto
Pessoa Junior and André Moreni Lopes.
