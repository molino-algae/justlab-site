---
title: S.A.C.I. wins Best Graduation Poster at the Industrial Biotechnology Week
date: 2026-10-23
tags: [award, open hardware]
---

Gabriela Tanaka Kiura, an undergraduate researcher in the JUST Lab, received the
award for **Best Graduation Poster** at the Industrial Biotechnology Week (SBI),
held on 19–23 October 2026. She presented **S.A.C.I. — Open Low-Cost Printed
Centrifugation System for Biotechnological Applications**.

S.A.C.I. is a low-cost centrifuge developed by 3D printing and based on the
principles of open hardware, with the aim of making laboratory equipment more
accessible for biotechnological applications. The project was supervised by João
Molino, with the Free Hardware Club and the BioSepNano Laboratory, and with the
support of Professor André Moreni Lopes.

A short video presents the project and its results:
[watch the video](https://lnkd.in/d3nAhjan). You can also read
[the original post on LinkedIn](https://lnkd.in/p/dvDSYUSy). Full details of the
design are on the S.A.C.I. page under Resources → Open hardware.
